# 0x00443FB0 (643 bytes) and 0x00444580 (60 bytes) — wave 14

(`cp -a matching2/msvc`); the landing was written back through

## The answer in one paragraph

**Both regions are recovered EXACT.** `0x00444580` is 60/60 and lands as a
selected function of the render-batch cluster with its two callees earned by
compiled ABI anchors; `0x00443FB0` is **643/643** — down from wave 13's 654 — and
lands the same way. The eleven bytes wave 13 was short were never the position
block's schedule on its own: they were the frame (0x1c against retail's 0x20),
which in turn came from three source-shape decisions *outside* the position
block, after which the position block itself fell out of a fourth. In order of
discovery:

1. `data_004978c0[0].color = color;` must sit **after** the distance test, not
   before it, with the faded channels written bytewise into the quad and the
   un-faded colour stored whole in the other arm. That alone took 664 → 643 and
   is what lets the store sink past the compare the way retail's does.
2. The saturation idiom must be `scaled >= 0x100 ? 0xFF : scaled`; wave 13's
   `(unsigned char)scaled` false arm costs a `movzx`/`jmp` pair per channel
   (10 bytes over four channels).
3. `span` must be folded **before** the colour select; that is what schedules
   retail's `fld [0x491e78]` into the `test ah,ah` shadow.
4. The distance fade is `(data_00491e78 - distance) / span`, not wave 13's
   reciprocal `span / (data_00491e78 - distance)`: the reciprocal emits `fdivp`
   where retail has `fdivrp`, and the guard reads `if (data_00491e78 < distance)`
   (emitting `fld C; fcomp st(1)`) where `distance > C` emits `fcom [C]`. Wave
   13's model was semantically inverted here while matching every other byte of
   the block.
5. The position block needs the `position + base` pairs summed into `delta`, the
   render offset added into a second vector `t`, and the focus subtracted back
   into `delta`. With that, the pre-subtraction sums live in `t`'s slots and the
   deltas in `delta`'s own, and retail's `fstp [esp+0xc]` / `fstp [esp+0x18]`
   pair comes out unchanged. That one instruction pair was the whole of wave
   13's remaining 643-byte delta.

The dispatcher **does not change at all** when these bodies join: it keeps its
216 bytes byte-for-byte, including case 6's `mov edi,eax; mov esi,ecx`. Its two
declarations were already ordered `(manager, entity)` — the order the pass
derives from these bodies — so nothing in `src/render/anm_vm_draw_dispatch.cpp`
had to move. Wave 13's two-byte regression was an artefact of declaring case 6
`(entity, manager)` to match its then-current 654-byte model.

## Levers that turned out to matter

1. **A frame-size residual is a source-shape residual.** 664 against 643 was
   10 bytes of saturation idiom, 4 bytes of missing frame and the store's
   position — all three had to be fixed before the position block's own two
   instructions could be seen at all.
2. **Store placement across a compare is decided by the source's arm structure.**
   With the bytewise write into the quad and the whole-colour store in the other
   arm the store sinks into the skip path exactly as retail's does; a single
   store in front of the test is kept in front of it.
3. **An inverted division is invisible in the instruction *sequence*.** Only the
   `fdivrp`/`fdivp` byte separates `span / (C - distance)` from
   `(C - distance) / span`, and only `fld C; fcomp st(1)` against `fcom [C]`
   separates `if (C < distance)` from `if (distance > C)`.
4. **`(manager, entity)` is the right declaration for both helpers.** With the
   final body, declaring `0x00443FB0` `(manager, entity)` gives retail's
   `entity EDI / manager ESI` *and* leaves the dispatcher byte-exact; wave 13
   reached for `(entity, manager)` only because its 654-byte body re-homed
   otherwise. No dispatcher line needed changing after all.
5. **An anchor's address is its whole point.** The `0x00444240` anchor must sit
   at 0x444240 for `0x00444580`'s `call` displacement, so while a candidate body
   was longer than 643 bytes the probe layout had to move that anchor; the
   interim `0x00444580` differences that caused are not regressions.
