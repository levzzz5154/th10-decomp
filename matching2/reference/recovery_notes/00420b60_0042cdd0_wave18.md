## Tooling notes for the next reader

* The multi-function layout works: `ui_state_accessors_00421c90` registers seven
  accessors of one record in one cluster, with the gap sections numbered even and
  the function sections odd so the linker places them in address order.
* `extern "C" __stdcall` contexts give predictable `_Name@N` symbols, but the
  layout's `context.symbol` must be the *undecorated* `Name@N` form (the linker
  applies the leading underscore itself, and the loader falls back to
  `'_' + symbol` in the map).
* The context contribution length is discovered by building once and reading the
  new `native-*/cluster.map` contribution table; a context address must sit at or
  after the last gap's end and must not overlap the function.
* Two shapes force retail's branch layout where the compiler would otherwise
  select branchlessly: `if (a || b) return 1; return 0;` for two tests sharing a
  return, and two forward `goto`s onto a labelled trailing return for two
  distinct constants (0x420B60, 0x429A30).
* A leaf whose body uses its receiver more than once (needing a separate temp)
  tends to keep it in ECX; single-load leaves get EAX. That is why the getters
  landed and 0x422200 did not.
