## The last residual: escaping selection words

The former 286/288 model hoisted loads of the two run-selection words above
the row-color store through the ASCII-manager pointer. Their separate extern
symbols had no visible address escape, allowing c2 to prove that the pointer
store could not affect them. In the complete game these words belong to the
global state whose address escapes.

`result_rows_context.cpp` retains the renderer as an independent root and
makes both addresses escape through an unselected helper. The renderer's
ordinary C++ body is unchanged. Native comparisons:

| Escape context | Instructions identical | Differing bytes |

| Neither word | 286/288 | 44 |
| 0x00474C68 only | 287/288 | 31 |

| Both words | 288/288 | 0 |

Escaping the score-pointer global instead regresses the schedule. A compiler
barrier after the color store also reached 287/288, but delayed that pointer
load; no barrier is present in the promoted source.
