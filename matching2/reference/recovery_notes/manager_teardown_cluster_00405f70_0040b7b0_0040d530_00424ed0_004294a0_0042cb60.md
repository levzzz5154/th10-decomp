## Semantic recovery and failed conversion evidence

Legacy-semantic source and retail Ghidra/disassembly agree on these behaviors:

* `0x00405F70` releases the two scheduler registrations, marks the item
  resource for animation-manager removal, clears the owner global, and invokes
  the EH vector destructor over 0x7D1 entries at manager offset `0x60`, stride
  `0x7F0`, using the item release destructor.
* `0x0040B7B0` removes two scheduler registrations, clears indexed animation
  resources, releases the manager-owned resource at `+0x18`, clears four
  animation-manager global slots at `0x3AD0E0..0x3AD0EC`, frees `+0x14`, and
  clears the manager globals.
* `0x0040D530` calls the enemy-list release dependency, removes both scheduler
  registrations, frees the pointer array entries at `+0x54`, releases/frees
  that array, conditionally clears four animation-manager slots beginning at
  `0x3AD090`, and updates the owner global.
* `0x00424ED0` removes both scheduler registrations, clears the stage global,
  conditionally marks the texture resource or releases the animation slot,
  frees `+0x45C` and `+0x36C`, and restores the SEH chain.
* `0x004294A0` frees manager resources at `+0x14` and `+0x18`, invokes the
  effect cleanup dependency eight times, frees eight `+0x1C` slots, removes
  the scheduler registrations at `+8`, `+0x1CC`, and `+0xC`, clears the owner
  when it matches, and runs the eight-element vector destructor at `+0xA0`
  with stride `0x24`.
* `0x0042CB60` resets the manager vtable, stops its worker, removes the two
  scheduler registrations, releases the two global animation slots, destroys
  and frees 0x32 effect-manager entries at `+0x59E4`, scans the animation
  lists for the manager resource, frees `+0x5AAC`, updates the owner, and
  installs the terminal vtable.

A bounded native-LTCG candidate was attempted first at `0x00405F70`, binding
the existing scheduler/animation helper ABIs, critical-section IATs, owner
globals, allocator, item destructor, and vector destructor.  The normalized
candidate contribution was only `0xB6` (182) bytes against the required `0xEC`
(236) bytes.  Its first bytes were:

```text
candidate: 8b 44 24 04 53 8b 1d b4 60 46 00 55 8b 2d e4 1b 49 00
retail:   64 a1 00 00 00 00 68 ff ff ff ff 68 3c 55 46 00 50 64 89 25
```

Thus it omitted the retail SEH registration and did not reproduce the retail
frameless/prologue/register-preservation shape.  The attempt was rejected on
extent before any byte promotion.  Because this is a shared teardown shape,
the other five bodies were not left with speculative semantic candidates.

## Next dependency

The next useful bounded cluster is an exact native-LTCG model for the common
critical-section wrapper and `Scheduler_Remove` custom ABI while retaining
each manager's distinct entry ABI: stack manager arguments for `0x00405F70`,
`0x0040B7B0`, `0x00424ED0`, and `0x004294A0`, versus the manager in `EAX` at
`0x0040D530`.  It must also model the retail SEH state/prologue and cleanup
paths, the EH vector destructor callback, and the allocator/resource helpers
`FUN_00447810`, `FUN_0042AB20`, `FUN_00409F90`, and the item/enemy release
dependencies.  Existing promoted animation-manager helpers may be consumed
read-only; their sources and shared tooling should remain outside this lane.
