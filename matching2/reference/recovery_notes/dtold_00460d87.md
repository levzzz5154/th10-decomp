# Binary64 to x87 extended conversion at `0x00460D87`

`___dtold` converts a binary64 value, passed by address as two little-endian
32-bit words, into the ten meaningful bytes of the CRT's x87 extended format.
The destination is four-byte aligned; the helper writes its low significand
dword at offset 0, high significand dword at offset 4, and sign/exponent word
at offset 8. The final two bytes of the caller's 12-byte temporary are left
untouched.

The semantic implementation follows the observed integer conversion:

- Either binary64 zero is canonicalized to ten zero bytes, including negative
  zero.
- Normal values copy the 52-bit fraction into the extended significand,
  insert the explicit integer bit, and add `0x3C00` to the binary64 exponent.
- Infinity and NaN use extended exponent `0x7FFF`, preserve the shifted
  fraction payload, and set the explicit integer bit.
- A nonzero binary64 subnormal starts at exponent `0x3C01` without the integer
  bit, then shifts the 64-bit significand left until normalized, decrementing
  the exponent for each shift.

## Ghidra evidence and ABI

Live Ghidra decompilation identifies a 186-byte `___dtold` with signature
`void __cdecl(uint*, uint*)`. Its only direct caller is
`FUN_00460E41` at `0x00460E41`. That caller pushes the contiguous input-word
address, then the address of its extended-value temporary, so the callee sees
destination first and source second. It calls `0x00460D87`, then removes both
arguments itself; this confirms two pointer arguments and cdecl cleanup. The
caller passes the resulting words to the decimal formatter as separate low
and high dwords plus the 16-bit sign/exponent field. Its three own callers and
record layout are described in
[`decimal_conversion_record_00460e41.md`](decimal_conversion_record_00460e41.md).

The C++ body uses integer shifts and masks only. It contains no inline
assembly, floating-point evaluation, external helper calls, or change to the
observed stack ABI. The shared header now declares the typed canonical entry
and the legacy raw alias. `regions.json` records the 186-byte region as
semantic and preserves the former assembly row and `_asm_dtold_00460d87`
binding.

No build, probe, or test was run.
