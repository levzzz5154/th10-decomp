C++ accepted as an equivalent recovery: the maintained probe compiles the body
in a native LTCG link and `equivalence.py` verifies 286 of 288 instructions

chosen row highlighted, filled slots formatted from the replay header with

## Nonobvious findings

1. **The busy flag is the queue's own `current_64`.** The retail entry writes
   1 at `g_pGameThread+0x8988` and 0 on the way out - the same word the queue
   writer stores into every record as `value_64`. No separate "busy" field
   exists; `GameThread_00401530::current_64` covers both.
2. **The case A/B difficulty strings are the initials table at `0x474708`**
   ("E","N","H","L","X"), not the difficulty-name table at `0x4746F4` the title
   browser uses for the same header field. A new extern
   (`data_00474708`) was needed.
3. **The selected-row color is `0xFFFFFF00`.** The retail `setne/dec/and
   0x7F7E80/add 0xFF808080` chain computes yellow against the gray
   `0xFF808080` base; my first reading of the constant arithmetic as
   `0xFFFF00FF` was wrong and produced the tell-tale mask `0x7E807F`.
4. **The case C condition reaches its date through a table pointer** for the
   `localtime` argument while the test and every argument spelling walks the
   whole `data_0047783c[...] .table.rows[diff][row]` path - the same shape the
   exact score-rows renderer at `0x004329F0` records. Caching the row pointer
   across `localtime` or spelling the empty-row arguments through it changes
   the addressing.
5. **Call sites: the position goes to the formatter in EBX**, thread, format
   and varargs on the stack; the compiler derives it from the complete
   formatter and queue-writer bodies in the same translation unit (the
   established lever). The dated rows evaluate `shot_type*3 + character`
   first, `difficulty` last, and the difficulty name uses the initials table.
6. **Residual difference.** In the ranking loop the two independent global
   loads (`0x00474C68`, `0x00474C6C`) are emitted above the unrelated color
   store where retail keeps them below it; the recorded
   `evaluation-order` hunks cover exactly those two instructions and nothing
   else. Dozens of statement-order, local and type shapes were probed first;
   the schedule survived all of them.

## 2026-10-17: the pointer-store edge, still parked

The `msvc-c2-pointer-store-adds-scheduler-edge` lever was tried on the ranking
loop's color store (the store through a pointer local, and the same at the
loop-exit store): the two counter loads stay above the store in both forms,
identical to the recorded three hunks. The loads are `+32`-priority nodes by
the scheduler formula (`msvc-c2-list-scheduler-priority`), so a one-point
edge on the store cannot outrank them; retail's schedule implies its loads
were not flagged the same way or the store's path is much longer. That needs
the scheduler trace to resolve. Region stays equivalent.
