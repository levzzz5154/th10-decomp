## Verified findings (fix in place, keep them)

- Timer head is `if (++front->banner_timer_9ecc >= 0x78)` — that spelling
  produces the retail `mov eax,ecx` compare copy and the sunk store.
- The mode word is `(gate & 0x1000 ? 0xFFFFFFF4u : 0) + 0xE` — the ternary
  produces retail's `and/neg/sbb/and/add`; the `(x != 0)` spelling does not.
- The lookup walks must be `__forceinline` expansions (FindTree), not calls:
  retail inlines the two-bucket walk at every user; calls leave ~1 KB behind.
- The six release slots are six explicit inlined expansions, not a rolled loop.
- Gauge numerator/denominator come from the script body (+0x23FC/+0x2400 of
  `seq->field_10`), not the play record.
- `data_00470d08` is a float (`fcomp dword`); `data_00470d00/d10` are doubles.
- `fabs(offset) < data_00470cf0` is the boss-alpha branch (no 0.0f compare,
  no `(double)` cast).
- Cluster `constant_aliases`: `__real@3f800000` → 0x00470AFC and
  `__real@3b800000` → 0x0044C000; without them the pooled 1.0f lands in the
  model's own pool and the ramp/compare bytes diverge.

## Residual differences (0x00414900, in retail order)

1. Prologue: retail defers `push ebx/ebp/esi` to 0x0041493C (right before the
   first bank loop); C2 emits them at entry, so the first block's `front`
   displacement is `[esp+0x18]` against retail `[esp+0xC]`. Everything else in
   the first 0x160 bytes then matches.
2. Retail hoists `mov ebp,[0x00474C4C]; mov esi,0x2710` above the gauge-bank
   `RunVM` call; C2 sinks both below it in the model.
3. Front pointer register: retail keeps the runtime in EDI for the whole
   body; the model reloads it into EBX after the digits block (register-choice
   class; colors many later rows, e.g. `[ebp+0]` vs `[eax]` encodings).
4. Ten-slot loop bound: model `jb 0xA` vs retail `jl 0xA`.
5. Remaining tail blocks (script position, boss fade, frame ramps) track
   within a few register/operand-order tie-breaks.

## Session-3 finding: the 0x00415E90 frame slots

Retail's create-call sret slots are per-site and distinct: op19's seven cases
allocate seven consecutive 8-byte homes ([esp+0xB8..0xE8] at equal push depth),
so MSVC 7.1 does not merge these locals across switch cases. The model already
reproduces per-site homes (0x24/0x28/0x2C/0x30/0x34/0x5C/0x38/0x54) but the
frame is 0x3C against retail 0xD8 — the gap is the NUMBER of locals and their
allocation order, not a merge problem. Next: inventory retail's [esp+X] uses
in order of first appearance (duration temp at +0x10 first, then the create
homes and the MarkText/path temporaries) and mirror that declaration/use order
in the dispatcher body so C2 lays the frame out the same way.

## Session-8 setge/sbb encoding experiments (negative result)
