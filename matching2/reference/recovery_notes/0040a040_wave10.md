## What the two placements are made of

Measured this wave, device by device, on the whole 234 bytes:

* **Delta 1 is one region boundary and two live ranges.** The EDI save is
  emitted at a *region boundary* inside the clear block, and the barriers are the
  only lever that moves it: with a barriered clear block the save lands either
  immediately **before** the pool-pointer `lea` or immediately **after** the
  `lea` and the load, never in between. A barrier before the pool declaration
  (or between the declaration and the load, or only before the declaration) puts
  it before the lea; a barrier between the load and the store puts it after the
  load. Concretely:
  * "family X" - `[bar][pool decl][bar][load][store]` gives
    `push lea load(const=eax, load=ecx)`: registers right, push 12 bytes early,
    **14**.
  * "family Y" - `[pool decl][load][bar][store]` gives
    `load lea push` (retail's order) but the mask materialises in the *next*
    region, where the load's value is a live-in of EAX, so the pair is
    load=EAX / mask=ECX: **15** (1 byte of prologue + 11 bytes of the
    mask/`and`/`mov [esi+6Ch]` group + the pop).
  * With no barrier at all the whole body is one region: the *content* is
    retail's (registers right, everything from `and ecx,eax` on byte-identical)
    but the region containing the lea starts at the function's first
    instruction, so **both** saves move to the top of the function and the lea
    sinks past the mask materialisation - 121 differing. Reproduced in the
    cluster (121), so the plain TU is not hiding anything here.
  * (A) and (B) cannot both hold: (1) needs the region holding the lea to end
    between the lea and the mask's materialisation, (2) needs the load, the
    mask's materialisation, the `and` and the store in one region - which puts
    the mask materialisation before that boundary. Every one of the ~3,000
    shapes measured lands on 14 or 15, never lower.
* **Delta 2 never moves.** 100+ tail shapes were built (below): in every one the
  restore keeps the slot it has now (directly after the flags store, one
  instruction later than retail). The only tail forms
  that do move something also reorder the first `rep stos` run
  (`mov ecx,0xEB` before `mov edi,esi`, 6 bytes) or fold the flags update into
  an in-place `or [edx],2` (230 bytes) - both worse.

## Shapes tried this wave (all measured)

1. **Barrier-slot cross products.** All 8 combinations of {leading, between
   pool-decl and load, between load and store} x {decl first, load first}
   (`s10b.py`, `bar/*.cpp`): 14 for every X combination, 15 for every Y one. A
   512-variant sweep over 7 slots that also put a barrier at two positions inside
   the element block and at two inside the memset block (`gen19.py`): best 14. A
   256-variant sweep adding "no barriers between the eight clears" and "no
   barrier before the memset" (`gen20.py`): the barrier **between the eight
   clears is load-bearing** (without it each of the eight clears splits into a
   load/and/store and the model grows to 298 bytes), while the barrier **before
   the memset is inert** - dropping it leaves the 14 bytes unchanged
   (`/tmp/StateInit/nombar.cpp`, 234/234, 17 raw = 14 post-link), so a future
   landing can carry a slightly smaller model. The best is still 14.
2. **Mask timing.** The mask declared before the pool pointer, after the load,
   before the barrier, as `mask`, as `0xFFFFFFFEu`, as a literal cast
   (`gen11.py`, `gen16.py`): the materialisation is always sunk to the first
   `and`, so the register pair never changes (15 for every Y-family shape, 14 for
   every X-family shape).
3. **AND before the barrier.** `masked = first & ~1u` in the load's region with
   the store after the barrier (A1..A15, `gen13.py`): the mask's live range then
   starts inside the element-block region, which displaces the header loop's EAX
   zero (9 bytes at `0x00..0x08`, the wave-8 delta (a) again) or - with a leading
   barrier - moves the push to the region start and sinks the lea past the mask
   (30). One useful datum: without a leading barrier the push then lands
   *interior* to its region (after the mask, before the `and`), which is the only
   interior placement seen, but it costs the preamble.
4. **Volatile first clear** (`*(volatile unsigned int *)word &= ~1u` and 9 more
   forms, `gen17.py`): the fences reorder the whole prefix, 115-131 differing.
5. **Inlining shape (lesson 2).** The whole clear group as one inlined
   `ClearAll(pool)`, the first clear as an inlined returning helper, the eight
   clears through a per-clear inlined setter, the memsets through a zeroing
   helper, the tail through `OrFlagPtr`/`WithBit`/`PublishAndFlag` accessors, and
   elements 1/2 through an inlined `InitSub(volatile SubState&)` called twice
   (`gen18.py`, `gen24.py`): byte-identical to the corresponding non-helper
   shape (helpers at 15, the per-clear setter variant 30, `hX` 14). Inlining
   shape moves neither placement.
6. **Element-block barriers.** A barrier at each of the 15 store positions x
   {X, Y, Ylead, plain} (`gen23.py`, `gen25.py`), with and without the
   all-fifteen-volatile element form: the barrier breaks the element block's own
   bytes (the header zero hoists / the stores re-group), 43+ differing. No
   combination improves on 14. The element block has to stay one straight run.
7. **Tail shapes.** 12 volatile/RMW/split/pointer forms (`gen14.py`), 13
   statement-order/scope/`do{}while(0)`/publish-first forms (`gen15.py`), 7
   volatile-store forms, 4 inlined tail helpers, and 6 barrier-bounded tails
   (`gen26.py`): every one leaves `pop edi` directly after `mov [edx],eax`;
   publish-first, plain (non-volatile) flags updates and the barrier-bounded
   tails additionally cost 6-11 bytes elsewhere.
8. **Randomized sweeps.** 1,200 samples over clear-group statement orders,
   mask declarations and fill-barrier patterns (`gen21.py`) and 2,000 samples
   over the declaration/load/mask/store forms with random barriers, a load-first
   ordering and the split-AND form (`gen22.py`): the best of all 3,200 is 14
   and none scores lower, so retail's prologue
   `[mov ecx][lea esi][push edi]` is not reachable through this space.
9. **Load-before-declaration family** (`gen23.py`, `map10.py`): `[load][bar]
   [decl][store]` puts the push after the load but sinks the lea past the mask
   (35); `[load][bar][decl][bar][store]` pins the lea but keeps the push before
   it (30); `[decl][load][bar][store]` is family Y (15).
10. **The no-barrier model** (item 1 above): only model whose *content* is
    retail's, blocked solely on where the two saves land and on the lea's sink.
    Worth a retry if a device is ever found that pins a mid-function save without
    a region boundary between the load and the mask's first use.

`0x40A130` is already landed, so neither is a blocker). The wave-7 note's
analysis of the factory's own ABI is unchanged.
