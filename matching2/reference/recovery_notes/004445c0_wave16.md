## The recovered body

`0x004445C0..0x00444751` projects the entity's quad through `0x00444240`
(entity in EAX, manager on the stack), then walks the four fade probes the
projection published at `manager+0x3ADA78` (stride `0x14`, `{x,y,z,u,v}`),
transforms each through `manager+0x3AD0F0` (the world matrix) with
`D3DXVec3Transform` (the `0x4521C4` thunk), takes its distance from the camera
focus at `0x491D7C`, and either copies the entity's colour (`color_secondary` at
`+0x300` when `flags & 0x8000`, else `color` at `+0x2FC`) straight into the
shared quad vertex, or attenuates its RGB toward the fade colour while keeping
the alpha. `near` is `0x491E78`, `far` `0x491E7C`, the per-channel fade targets
are the floats `0x491E80/84/88`, the packed far colour is `0x491E90`, and the
span is `near - far`. It finishes with `SubmitSpriteQuad(entity, 2)` and then
stores `1.0f` into the four quad `rhw` slots back to front.

Three source shapes are load-bearing and were measured against the bytes:

1. **The colour reduction is float, not int.** Retail's per-channel body is
   `fild <channel>; fsubs <target float>; fmul; call __ftol2` — the *target is a
   float* here, unlike sibling `0x00443FB0`, whose target is read as
   `(int)data_00491e80` and therefore emits `flds 0x491e80; call __ftol2` first.
   The last two channels must be written from one 16-bit read of the colour's
   high half: spelled as two independent byte loads the compiler emits
   `mov cl,[esp+0x12]; mov dl,[esp+0x13]; sub cl,al; mov [esi-1],cl; mov [esi],dl`
   (one byte longer, 403 total); as `unsigned short tail` it emits retail's
   `mov cx,[esp+0x12]; mov dl,cl; sub dl,al; mov [esi-1],dl; mov [esi],ch`.
2. **The guard is `if (data_00491e78 < distance) … else …`**, giving retail's
   `fld C; fcomp st(1); test ah,5; jp <unfaded>` and the `fsubr`/`fdiv` chain
   (`C - distance` over the span) — the same guard spelling wave 14 landed in
   `anm_distance_color.cpp`.
3. **The anchor is not needed**: the projection `0x00444240` and the submission
   `0x00442670` are still the cluster's `ProjectedEntity_00444240::ProjectQuad`
   anchor and the recovered `AnimationManager_00442F50::SubmitSpriteQuad`, and
   the body calls both exactly as its siblings do.

## The 9 bytes: a codegen tie-break, not a source property

The two instructions are independent: `lea edi,[esp+0x1c]` materialises the
transform's output array and `mov esi,0x4978d3` the shared quad's colour byte
pointer; neither feeds the other (the third init, `add ebp,0x3ada78`, is
identical in both). Swapping them is semantically inert, and every measure says
the order is decided by the MSVC 13.10 list scheduler **after** the source shape
is gone.

**Shapes tried (all measured; the region's emitted size and the preheader order
are the observation).** In the cluster (12 s/run, real `/GL` link): the baseline
index loop; a vertex-pointer loop; a hoisted `out` pointer assigned inside;
hoisted pointers stepped in the `for` clause; `unsigned int*` + byte alias;
`(unsigned char*)data_004978c0 + i*28 + 16`; `float projected[4][4]`; a hoisted
probe pointer; `i++` / `++i`; a count-down loop; two index variables; `do/while`;
`int i` declared outside the `for`; `4 > i`; `i += 1`; declaring `out` first in
the body; the arms swapped (`if (near > distance) store else fade`, which changes
the compare idiom to `test ah,0x41; jne`); a direct manager expression instead of
the `view` local; the module order in `additional_sources`. In the plain `/O2`
oracle (0.12 s/run — it agrees with the cluster on every shape cross-checked):
fifteen further code-neutral perturbations (two-statement span, if/else colour
select, colour/span reorder, inline `sqrt`, cast destination, casts,
`const` locals, `char*`, `unsigned long`, block scoping) plus `_ReadWriteBarrier`
after the quad statement, before the loop and after the transform. **Every one of
them emits `lea edi` first, exactly like the baseline**; only shapes that change
the loop *form* move the order, and each of those changes other bytes too
(a source-stepped pointer loop gives retail's `mov esi, lea edi, add ebp` order
but a `jb` test and no `+3` displacement fold — 402 bytes, 40 differing bytes; a
vertex-pointer loop folds the displacement but converts the loop to `dec/jne`
with the count spilled — 420 bytes).

**Corpus census** (the check wave 16's 0x443080 work asked for). Scanning
retail's `.text` for a `mov r32, <global address>` immediately followed or
preceded by a `lea r32,[esp+disp8]`:

| order | count | addresses |
|---|---|---|
| `mov reg,addr` then `lea reg,[esp+d]` | 3 | 0x403AC3, 0x439B83, **0x444603** (this region) |
| `lea reg,[esp+d]` then `mov reg,addr` | 5 | 0x420ADE, 0x420BCE, 0x4392B8, 0x439B7F, 0x44A5C0 |

Retail emits **both** orders, and this region sits in the minority — so the order
is not a fixed compiler preference but a per-function tie-break, which is what
three sibling workers found independently in this session: `DispatchCase8` for
0x00444760's `fld`/`movs` preheader ("decided by the /LTCG fixpoint… ~35
bisection builds… unreachable"), `BufferSeek` for 0x004436F0's two tail copies
("~200 source shapes reproduce the same order byte for byte"), and wave 15's
0x0042A3E0 (4 rel8 displacements, inert to everything). Source spelling is not
the lever; the two values' live ranges and their loop-carried phi are, and any
change that makes one of them live across the loop also changes the epilogue.

**Conclusion for the next wave: do not re-attempt this pair by respelling the
model.** Either find the /LTCG-context lever that moves a loop-carried phi (a
different caller shape in `render_batch_context.cpp` was tried by
`DispatchCase8` for its own region and did not move it), or leave 0x004445C0
`asm`. The model, the layout and the driver deltas above are reproduced in
`/tmp/DispatchCase7/msvc` (`iterate.py` rebuilds the cluster with an adaptive
declared size; `bdiff.py` prints the byte runs; `fast.py`/`shapes.py`/`scan.py`
are the plain-`/O2` shape oracle).
