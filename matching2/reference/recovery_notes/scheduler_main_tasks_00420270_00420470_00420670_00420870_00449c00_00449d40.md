## ABI/layout findings

- `0x00420270` receives its shutdown-state object in EAX; retail immediately
  copies it to EDI and reads object fields at `+0x0C`, `+0x10`, and `+0x14`.
  It has the two `0x0044C150` frame-marker calls, the `0x004203F0` cleanup,
  manager vtable releases, timer teardown through `0x004203C0`, and archive
  close `0x00434D10`. Its six dynamic/vtable paths must be modeled before
  compiler shape can be meaningful.
- `0x00420470` has no source-level arguments. It primes globals at
  `0x00491FB4`, `0x00491FB8`, and `0x00491FC0`, then creates four callback
  nodes. The callback addresses are `0x0041FF80`, `0x00420000`,
  `0x004200C0`, and `0x004200D0`; priorities are 1, 1, 0x28, and 0x32.
  Retail sets the task flag at +4, owner at +0x20, and the first callback's
  replace hook at +0x0C. The register callees use task in ESI, priority in
  EDI, scheduler on the stack, and return via `RET 4`.
- `0x00420670` receives the screenshot object in ESI and the filename in EAX.
  It waits on `+0x50C`, calls the surface vtable at +0x48 and +0x34/+0x38,
  constructs a 0x36-byte BMP header, allocates 0x2C and 0xE1000 bytes, and
  uses the 640x480 bottom-up 4-to-3-byte copy before starting the writer
  thread at `0x00420540`. The lock/unlock and allocator dependencies are
  mandatory for a useful LTCG context.
- `0x00420870` has the first stack object/formal visible through EBP and the
  filename held in EBX; its caller pushes the `0x00491C28` object and returns
  through `RET 4`. It copies 13 dwords from the loaded image before validating
  six mode bytes, magic `0x100003`, and size `0x34`; accepted keys publish to
  `0x00474E88..0x00474E98`, then the runtime flag echo and `0x0044B540`
  write-back run. The exact `0x0044B360` file-loader ABI and logger/write
  callees are the next needed dependencies.
- Both scheduler bodies enter/leave the critical section through the IATs at
  `0x004660B0`/`0x004660B4`, update byte `0x0049231C`, save each node's next
  link before callback execution, and call `0x00449F60` for removal. Primary
  starts from device +0x18 (head +0x14); secondary starts from +0x3C (head
  +0x38). Secondary does not implement primary's status-6 restart or status-7
  hook behavior.
