## What the region needed

The ABI is the same internal convention its already-landed sibling
`0x0042C620` uses (`layout/ltcg/title_options_timer_0042c620.json`): the timer
state in **EAX**, the only argument in **ECX**, plain `RET`. The
array-walking synthetic caller in the driver earns it; nothing else moved.

What was still missing was the schedule of the two leading stores. The wave-9
attempt left this region as assembly with exactly this residual - "the compiler
preserved the 83-byte body but delayed the leading `xor edx,edx` until its
first use, changing the retail instruction order"
(`title_options_menu_0042d920.md`). Reproduced here: the faithful body emits
retail's 83 bytes but with 17 differing bytes, all in the prologue:

```text
got                                    retail
42c5c0  mov  [eax+1Ch],ecx              xor  edx,edx
42c5c3  mov  ecx,[eax+2C0h]             mov  [eax+1Ch],ecx
42c5c9  xor  edx,edx                    mov  [eax+20h],edx
42c5cb  test cl,1                       mov  ecx,[eax+2C0h]
42c5ce  mov  [eax+20h],edx              test cl,1
42c5d1  jne  +0x29                      jne  +0x29
```

i.e. the second store sinks into the `test cl,1` / `jne` compare slot and the
zero is materialised at its use instead of at the top of the block. Everything
from `jne` (offset `+0x11`) to `ret` is already identical.

Declaring the owner and the pending target as one two-int group written through
a base pointer removes the sink, because the two stores then share one address
value instead of being two independent constant-offset stores: **0 differing
bytes**. Reordering statements, giving the group an array member, wrapping the
pair in a nested struct, or forcing a data dependency between the two stores
all reproduce the 17-byte residual; only the shared base steers it.

## Tooling note

`matching2/tools/waves/cluster_attempt.py` cannot drive this region as it
stands: its `_map_offset` / `_map_section` regexes match the *length* column of
the map's contribution table, so the adaptive retry writes a context address of
`0x00401047` and a zero-length gap, which then fails C2466; its layout writer
also hardcodes the `.text$01accessor` section name. The iteration here used a
local copy of the same build/compare loop that reads the segment:offset column
and re-converges the layout, and a zero-length gap is never written.
