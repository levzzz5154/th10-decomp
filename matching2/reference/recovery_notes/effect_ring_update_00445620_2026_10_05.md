## The x87 operand order

Every `fld A; fadd B` pair here is ordered by the hash class (`id & 3`) of the
address temporaries of the two operands (see the operand-order memory note and
`reference/compiler_traces/operandsort/`). What moves the classes:

- An inline accessor returning a value allocates one temporary at its position.
  `state->Rate()` in the loop's second countdown statement and
  `state->RadiusStep(index)` in the radius statement are what put the loop
  entries and the vector temporaries in the right classes.
- Statements outside the loop are numbered after the loop body. Their ids move
  by +1 per extra named local and by -1 when the radius is written as a
  compound assignment (`radii_a[index] += ...`) without a `radius` local.
- Spelling of the vector operators, constructor, colour and alpha statements
  does not move anything. Swapping `base + position` flips all three pairs.
