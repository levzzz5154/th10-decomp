## Batch flush evidence

The legacy semantic source and header identify the staged-quad count, cursor,
batch-start pointer, draw counter, and the four device leaves. Retail
disassembly at `0x00442F50` confirms the device global at `0x00491C30`, vtable
slots `+0x10C`, `+0x164`, and `+0x14C`, the manager offsets
`+0x3ADAC8`, `+0x72DACC`, `+0x72DAD0`, and `+0x58`, and the ESI receiver.
The exact source shape uses the outer `result = quad_count; if (result != 0)`
form and commits `batch_start`, `quad_count`, then `draw_calls`; that ordering
is required for the final 16-byte tail schedule.

An earlier equivalent source order produced the full 133-byte extent but 16
different bytes at `0x00442FC2..0x00442FD3`: it stored the draw counter before
the batch pointer and quad clear. Reordering those three semantic assignments
produced the exact body above.

## Color-slot probe and blocker

The candidate followed the legacy semantic source, Ghidra pseudocode, and the
IDA listing in `matching2/build/waves/results2/Y06/refs/entity_color_slot.txt`:
the +`0x1BC..0x204` color/state layout, lazy +`0x1F8` timer-scale pointer,
`0xFFF0BDC1` first-call sentinel, and `0xFFFFFFFF` active sentinel were all
carried into the member body.

Two focused native-LTCG contexts were tried and then removed:

1. A two-input pointer-array caller produced a 192-byte member body. It began
   `89 88 00 02 00 00` instead of retail's
   `83 EC 0C 53 55 56`; the compiler allocated value to ECX, the first color
   to ESI, and the second color to EDX, and ended with `RET 4` instead of the
   retail `RET 8`.
2. A five-array parallel caller (and a local-temporary spelling of the same
   call) retained the 192-byte result in this focused build. The archived
   wave-7 parallel-array experiment independently recorded a 208-byte body,
   but with value in ECX, first color in EDX, second color in ESI, and still
   `RET 4`; this is the same unresolved custom split, not an exact candidate.

The retail convention is object in EAX, first/second color pointers in ECX/EDX,
two values on the stack, and `RET 8`. No retained candidate met both extent and
bytes, so `entity_color_slot.cpp` was restored byte-for-byte to its production
asm source.

## Ring initializer probe and blocker

The candidate was built from the legacy ring semantics and the retail listing:
free any prior `owner+0x358` block, allocate `0x4B0`, install callbacks at
`+0x398/+0x39C`, seed the base-speed floats through the fixed RNG leaf at
`0x0044BB90`, and call the fixed polar leaf at `0x004458B0` through a member
ABI declaration. Absolute bindings covered malloc/free, RNG/polar, and the
referenced float data addresses.

The pointer-array context linked a 334-byte body (retail extent is 803). Its
first bytes were `51 8B 86 58 03 00 00 85 C0 74 13`, while retail begins
`8B 87 58 03 00 00 83 EC 28 85 C0 74 13`: the candidate received the owner
in ESI and emitted a receiver save, while retail receives the owner in EDI and
allocates the 0x28-byte x87 scratch frame. A single-owner context reduced only
the synthetic caller from 42 to 16 bytes; the recovered body remained 334
bytes with the same ESI receiver. The candidate also did not reach the retail
inner xorshift/random-data loop, so its extent could not be accepted on a
semantic-prefix basis.

The ring candidate and both context variants were removed; the production
803-byte asm body in `effect_ring.cpp` is unchanged. The fixed `fsincos` leaf
at `0x004458B0` remains an external dependency for any future attempt.

## Cleanup and next dependency

All focused native build directories under `matching2/build/render-effect-*`
were deleted. `git diff --check` is clean for the three owned live sources;
the entity-color and ring sources have no worktree diff. The next bounded
dependency for the two failures is a compiler/context route that can express
the exact custom register split (EAX/ECX/EDX plus stack/RET 8 for color) and
the EDI owner plus full x87 scratch/register schedule and xorshift loop for the
ring. No manifest promotion was made in this task.
