# 0x00407DA0 / 0x00407E40 playfield wraps

`WrapHorizontal` (148 bytes) and `WrapVertical` (172 bytes), promoted from the
anchor models in `bullet_runtime_caller`.

Two things were wrong with the models:

- They shared the timer/sound tail through a `goto`. Retail saves ESI/EDI
  before the first comparison and merges the two position stores, which is
  what the compiler emits when the tail is written out in both branches and
  tail-merged.
- The bounds were `extern float` globals, which the compiler loads first in
  `extent + bound`. Written as literals (384, ±192, 448, and a double 0.0 for
  the top edge) the member is loaded first, as in retail. The literals are
  bound to the retail constants through `data_aliases`.
